A text to read with a pencil in hand

This is the textbook for the Basic Geometry course in an interactive version. It has the same content as the printed book, but it is designed for studying on screen:

References that unfold
When you click the number of a cited theorem, its statement appears right there, so you never lose the thread.
Folded proofs
Try the proof before opening it. The solutions of the examples can be hidden too.
Figures that move
In the interactive figures you drag the points and the measurements update live.
Test yourself
Self-grading questions at the end of the sections. Your progress is saved in this browser.

The tree on the cover is a Pythagoras tree: each right triangle carries a square on each leg, and the process repeats. Move the controls to watch it grow.
